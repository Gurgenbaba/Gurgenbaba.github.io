How ABBES Digital uses AI.

ABBES Digital uses AI-assisted tools for development, analysis, prototyping, testing and, in some cases, for creating or editing drafts. AI is a tool; publication, technical decisions and accountability remain human.

1. Published content

Text, design and software released by ABBES Digital or delivered to clients are reviewed by a human before release and published under ABBES Digital's responsibility. AI assistance during drafting, writing or development therefore does not automatically mean that every item must carry a visible “AI-generated” label.

2. Direct AI interaction

If an ABBES product offers a direct conversation with an AI system, AI agent or comparable system, users are informed clearly from the beginning of the interaction unless the AI nature is already obvious.

3. Synthetic image, audio and video

Synthetic or manipulated media is assessed per project, especially where real people, places, objects, organisations or events are depicted in a way that could falsely appear authentic. Fictional game art and stylised concepts are not automatically treated as deepfakes.

4. AI in development and testing

AI may assist with programming, refactoring, testing, technical analysis and prototyping. Architecture, review, security decisions and release remain under human responsibility. Development assistance is not presented as a direct AI interaction with website visitors.

5. External AI services and privacy

If personal data is sent to an external AI provider, that processing is assessed separately under data-protection law and, where required, described in the product's privacy notice. Not every project uses external AI services at runtime.

6. Project-level transparency

Active projects maintain a technical AI compliance baseline. Features such as AI chat, generative runtime output, synthetic voice/image/video, emotion recognition or biometric categorisation trigger a renewed compliance review before release.

Legal framework

The relevant framework includes Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act). Article 50 transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026. This page documents ABBES Digital's technical practice and is not individual legal advice.

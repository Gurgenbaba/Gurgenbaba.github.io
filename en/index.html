About

Behind Gurgenbaba.

Gurgenbaba is my label as a developer. I build my own browser games and systems and deliver web projects for clients, from concept to live operation.

You work directly with the person writing the code. No agency in between, short paths, honest assessments.

I work with AI tools — openly, not hidden. For you, that means faster prototypes, more thorough testing and more time for the decisions that matter in the end. Architecture, review and responsibility for the code stay with me.

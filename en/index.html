FAQ

Quick answers.

What will my project cost?

Guide prices are listed under Pricing above: small jobs from €39, online business cards from €390, websites from €890, online shops from €1,490. After a free intro call you get a binding fixed price. The request itself is non-binding.

How do I pay?

For projects, half at the start and the rest on approval, by bank transfer or PayPal. Small jobs are paid once they are done.

Is there funding?

For larger projects in Thuringia, yes. The Digitalbonus Thüringen covers up to 50% (at most €15,000) of digitalisation projects by small and medium-sized businesses with costs of at least €5,000, for example an online shop, a booking system or a customer portal. A plain website without such features is not eligible. The application has to be filed with the Thüringer Aufbaubank before the project starts. I can help with the concept it requires.

How long does it take?

The timeline is part of the quote. While I build, you see regular progress on a preview link instead of a surprise at the end.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing perfect. Bullet points are enough: what it should do, examples you like, and your logo, texts and images if you have them. We work out the rest together.

Can I edit content myself later?

Yes, if you want to. You then manage texts, products or bookings in an admin area, no code needed.

Do you handle hosting and updates too?

If you like: hosting, domain, updates, backups and extensions after launch, one-off or ongoing.

How do you use AI?

Openly and deliberately: AI tools speed up prototypes and testing. Architecture, review and responsibility for the code stay with me.

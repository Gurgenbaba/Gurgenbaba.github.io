Frequently asked questions

Questions about working together.

The key points on pricing, process, support and future development at a glance.

What costs should I expect for a project?

The amounts listed under Pricing are guide prices. Once goals, scope and requirements are clear, you receive a concrete quote with clearly described deliverables and costs. The initial enquiry is non-binding.

Do you also take on smaller jobs?

Yes. Technical fixes, content changes, individual extensions and improvements to existing websites can all be requested. If the budget is smaller, scope is reduced sensibly — not the quality of the implementation.

What does the collaboration process look like?

After your enquiry, we clarify the goal, scope and key conditions. You then receive an assessment and, if the project is a good fit, a concrete quote. During development you can review preview versions and give feedback early.

How long does implementation take?

That depends on scope and current availability. Smaller tasks can often be scheduled at short notice. Larger projects receive a realistic timeline before work begins and that timeline is documented in the quote.

What content do I need to prepare?

For the first conversation, bullet points about your business, the required functions and the goal of the project are enough. Existing logos, copy, images or references can be attached directly. We clarify the remaining details together.

Can I manage content myself or extend the system later?

Yes. If required, you can receive a simple admin area for content, products or bookings. The technical foundation can also be chosen so later additions such as booking, client areas, integrations or internal tools remain possible.

Do you also handle hosting, updates and ongoing support?

Yes, if required. This can include managed hosting, technical updates, backups, availability checks and later extensions. Scope and response times are agreed to suit the project.

How does ABBES Digital use AI?

AI-assisted tools are used deliberately for prototyping, analysis, testing and routine tasks. Architecture, technical decisions, review and responsibility for the delivered solution remain with ABBES Digital.
